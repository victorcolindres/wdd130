Our Trips

Los Dientes del Diablo

Difficulty: Class IV — Advanced

Duration: Full Day (8 hrs)

Price: $120 per person

Our most thrilling trip. Navigate fierce rapids through dramatic canyon walls. Only for experienced rafters or those with nerves of steel. Expert guides lead every run. Minimum age 16.

Big Mallard

Difficulty: Class III — Intermediate

Duration: Half Day (4 hrs)

Price: $75 per person

A perfect balance of excitement and accessibility. Big Mallard offers rolling waves and fun rapids through beautiful forested stretches. Great for first-timers looking for a real challenge. Minimum age 12.

Rapid Rush

Difficulty: Class II — Beginner

Duration: 2 hrs

Price: $45 per person

New to rafting? Start here. Rapid Rush eases you into the sport with gentle currents, small waves, and stunning riverside scenery. Family-friendly and suitable for all ages. No experience needed.

Wild River Overnight

Difficulty: Class III — Intermediate

Duration: 2 Days / 1 Night

Price: $195 per person

The ultimate outdoor experience. Raft through Class III rapids during the day and camp on the riverbank at night. Includes all camping gear, meals, and a guide throughout. Minimum age 14.
