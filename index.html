El Salvador

El Salvador is a beautiful country located in Central America, known for its impressive volcanoes, tropical climate, and stunning beaches along the Pacific Ocean. The capital city, San Salvador, is the economic and cultural center of the country. El Salvador has a rich history influenced by indigenous traditions and Spanish heritage, which can be seen in its architecture, food, and customs.
